# Trial

Try Hive free for 14 days: 10 nodes, 10 users, and no limit on apps, functions
or previews, for one installation. It takes no card. A trial is not a
subscription: nothing is charged, and there is nothing to cancel.

## Start a trial

1. In **System → License**, click **Start a 14-day trial**. The button shows
   while no key has been installed, and opens the license portal's trial page
   with the installation ID filled in.
2. Sign in to the portal, or create an account with your email address: the
   portal emails you a link to finish creating it.
3. Check the **Installation ID**. The first time, give the **Name on the
   license**, yours or your company's. Click **Start the trial**.
4. The portal shows the key, emails it, and keeps it under **Licenses**. Copy it.
5. Paste it into **System → License**, under **Install a key**: see
   [Install a license key](./install-a-license-key.md#paste-it).

**System → License** then shows **Hive**, with the status **Trial** and the day
it ends. A banner counts the days down, such as `Hive trial: 13 days left.`

The portal's own pricing page has **Try it free for 14 days** under Hive too: it
asks for the installation ID.

## One per installation, one per account

An installation has one trial, and so does an account. A second one is refused:
`This installation has already had a trial`, or
`This account has already had its trial`.

A trial stays with the installation it was started for: it cannot be
[moved](./moving-a-license.md).

## When it ends

A trial has no grace period. When its 14 days are over, the installation is on
Bee. Nothing that runs is stopped: an installation with more than Bee's limits
keeps everything, and can add nothing more of that kind. See
[Grace, expiry and limits](./grace-expiry-and-limits.md).

## Buy at any time

Subscribe to any plan, during the trial or after it, with **Upgrade** in
**System → License**: see [Buying and billing](./buying-and-billing.md). The
paid key replaces the trial's. The checkout shows it, and emails it, to paste.
With [online renewal](./renewal-and-privacy.md#online) on, an installation still
on its trial key gets the paid one by itself at its next daily check, or at once
with **Check for a newer key**.
